This Privacy Policy explains what personal data LONDRE AI LLC ("Londre", "we", "us") collects when you use the Londre mobile application and this website, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is LONDRE AI LLC, a limited liability company registered in Georgia, with its registered office at Commercial space N7, Floor 1, N 18 Kobaladze Street, Batumi 6010, Autonomous Republic of Adjara, Georgia. You can contact us at official@londre.ge.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account information

  • Mobile phone number, used to create and sign in to your account
  • First name and last name
  • Email address
  • City
  • Profile photograph, if you choose to upload one
  • Saved addresses you add to your profile
  • Language preference

2.2 Service and order information

  • Pickup and drop-off addresses and their map coordinates
  • Vehicle type and any additional service options you select
  • Scheduled date and time, where you book in advance
  • Estimated and final price, payment method and payment status
  • Photographs of the vehicle, if you choose to attach them to an order
  • Notes you add to an order
  • Ratings and written reviews you leave after a completed service

2.3 Location data

With your permission, the application uses your device location to show service providers near you and to give a recovery operator the correct pickup point. Where a recovery order is in progress, the assigned operator's location is shared with you so you can follow their approach. You can withdraw location permission at any time in your device settings; some features will not work without it.

2.4 Provider information

If you register as a service provider, we additionally collect business details and verification documents, which may include identification documents, a driving licence, vehicle registration, a business licence and vehicle insurance. These are used solely to verify that a provider is entitled to offer the service and are reviewed by our administration team.

2.5 Technical information

  • A push notification token for the device you last signed in on
  • Diagnostic and crash information, used to identify and fix defects

3. Why we use it

  • To create and maintain your account and authenticate you
  • To receive, dispatch, track and complete the services you order
  • To calculate prices and process payment
  • To send you notifications about the status of your orders
  • To verify service providers before they accept work
  • To handle support requests, complaints and disputes
  • To detect and prevent misuse, fraud and abuse of the platform
  • To comply with obligations under the law of Georgia

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We share only what is necessary, and only with:

  • The service provider assigned to your order — your name, contact number, pickup and drop-off location and the details of the job, so that the service can be carried out.
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — the application runs on Google Firebase for authentication, database, storage, notifications and diagnostics.
  • Mapping providers — Google Maps Platform for maps, geocoding, routing and distance calculation.
  • Payment providers — Georgian banking payment providers process card payments. Card details are entered with the payment provider and are not stored by Londre.
  • Competent authorities — where we are required to disclose information under applicable law.

5. Where data is stored

Data is stored on infrastructure operated by our cloud providers, which may process data outside Georgia. Where data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers under their own data processing terms.

6. How long we keep it

Account data is kept for as long as your account is active. Order records, including prices and payment status, are retained after completion for accounting, dispute resolution and legal compliance purposes. When you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile and personal identifiers; records that we are required to keep by law are retained for the required period and then deleted.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal data we hold about you and provide a copy
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw a permission you previously gave, such as location or notifications

Write to official@londre.ge. Where possible, send your request from the email address linked to your account so that we can verify it. We respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Security

Access to personal data is restricted to authorised personnel and to the systems that need it to operate the service. Administrative actions taken on accounts and orders are logged. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

9. Children

The Londre application is not directed at children. Accounts are intended for individuals who are legally able to enter into a contract in Georgia. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Where a change is significant, we will draw attention to it in the application.
